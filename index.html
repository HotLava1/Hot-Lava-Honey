Sweetness With Substance

A golden gift from Hawaiian blossoms.

Hot Lava Honey carries the fragrance and character of Kaʻū’s remarkable landscape. Beyond its lush flavor, honey contains naturally occurring plant compounds and offers a simple, time-honored way to add sweetness, comfort and quick energy to everyday moments.

✦

Naturally occurring antioxidants

Honey contains phenolic compounds and flavonoids associated with antioxidant activity. Their amounts and character vary with the flowers, geography and season that shaped each harvest.

🌺

Floral character from Hawaiʻi

ʻŌhiʻa lehua and the diverse blossoms of Kaʻū give Hawaiian honey its sense of place—an aromatic, nuanced sweetness that can make a small drizzle feel genuinely special.

☀️

Quick, natural energy

Honey’s naturally occurring carbohydrates provide readily available energy. Enjoy a spoonful before a busy morning, outdoor adventure or whenever you want a bright lift of sweetness.

🍵

A soothing kitchen ritual

Stirred into warm tea or taken by the spoonful, honey can feel wonderfully soothing on an occasional scratchy throat. It is comfort food in its simplest form.

🥄

A little goes a long way

Its full-bodied flavor brings depth to fruit, yogurt, toast, marinades and dressings. Because honey is still a source of sugar, savoring it in modest amounts is the sweetest approach.

Good to know: Honey is a food, not a treatment or cure. Never give honey to a child younger than 12 months because of the risk of infant botulism. Individual honey composition varies by floral source and harvest.

Learn more from the National Library of Medicine and CDC infant-feeding guidance.
