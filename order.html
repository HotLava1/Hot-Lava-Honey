Before You Order

Good to know.

Is payment due when I submit the form?

No. The form prepares an order-request email. Wait for confirmation of availability, fulfillment and the final total before paying.

Can I choose pickup or shipping?

Yes. Select local pickup or request a shipping quote in the customer-details form. Any applicable charge will be confirmed directly.

When should I use Venmo?

Use Venmo only after Hot Lava Honey confirms your order and final total.

Is my request automatically accepted?

Orders remain requests until availability and fulfillment details are confirmed directly by Hot Lava Honey.
